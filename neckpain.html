Osteopathic Care for Neck Pain in Etobicoke

Can Osteopathic Care Help With Neck Pain?

People with neck pain or stiffness may seek osteopathic care for an individualized hands-on assessment and treatment approach. The neck is similar to the chimney of a house in structural terms. It sits atop the roof, and its level is (often) dependent on the foundation of the house. The osteopathic assessment and treatment lets us discern where the causations stem from, i.e.; the neck itself or the lower portions of the body.

How Your Neck Assessment Works

The assessment looks at the way the body is functioning as a connected system before treatment is chosen. As stated above, the neck sits atop of the pelvic and spinal foundation of the body. It must therefore be considered along with the lower structures of the body. The neck is connected to the pelvis through fascia and long muscles. An issue in the lower body may create neck irritation.

A Gentle Approach to Treatment

Treatment is gentle and rhythmic, with no attempted forceful cracking or violent motions. The neck is a sensitive area due to the powerful nerves passing through it. It must therefore be handled and treated appropriately. Since the Osteopathic philosophy aims to work with the patient’s body, motions are not forced but aimed to relax the tissues with as little pressure as possible.
