Book An Osteopathy Appointment

Initial Osteopathy Manual Therapy appointment

This appointment is designed for the patient and Osteopathic Manual Therapist (OMT) to meet. The OMT will listen carefully to their health history, learn about the patients’ goals and expectations, and answer any questions they may have. This appointment includes an assessment and treatment portion. The appointment time is scheduled for 45 (forty-five) minutes; however appointments may conclude before the 45-minute mark.

Follow-Up Osteopathy Manual Therapy appointment

This appointment is designed for the OMT to continue working with the patient with the focus being on assessment and treatment. The appointment time is scheduled for 30 (thirty) minutes; however appointments may conclude before the 30-minute mark.
