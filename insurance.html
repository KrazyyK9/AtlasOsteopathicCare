Insurance and Extended Health Benefits

Is Osteopathic Care Covered by Insurance?

Osteopathic care may be covered through extended health benefits, but coverage depends on each patient's individual plan and provider.

Common Insurance Questions

Direct Billing

Does Atlas Osteopathic Care offer direct billing? No. Payment is taken after the session, and you are e-mailed a receipt. This is to be submitted to your insurance provider.

Ongoing Motor Vehicle Accident (MVA)

If you are currently involved in an ongoing motor vehicle accident (MVA) claim, please inform your OMT. You will not be allowed to be treated until the claim has ended. Proof, or a note, will need to be provided.

Missed Appointments

If you miss an appointment and are charged, your claim cannot and will not be processed through insurance. This is fraud.
